eleventy-plugin-netlify-data-store Read & write build-time content for Eleventy from Netlify Blobs, with a TTL-based local cache for fast, offline-tolerant builds

Build-time content from Netlify Blobs, cached locally with a TTL you control, so builds stay fast, survive an outage, & never fail on missing data.
